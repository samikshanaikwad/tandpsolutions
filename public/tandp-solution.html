Manufactured Systems

Products & SPM Machine Manufacturing

Factory-tested electrical control panels and custom special purpose machines built to your specific industrial requirements.

AUTOMATION PANEL

PLC Panels (Automation Panel)

Engineered for deterministic industrial controller operations, isolated I/O relay banks, and door-mounted operator HMIs.

• Ingress Rating: IP54 / IP55 Powder Coated CRCA

• Busbar & Power: 24V DC / 230V AC / 415V AC

MOTOR CONTROL

MCC (Motor Control Centre)

Centralized motor starters (DOL, Star-Delta, Soft Starter) with microprocessor protection relays and compartmentalized feeders.

• Ingress Rating: Form 3b / Form 4 Separation

• Rating: 415V AC 3-Phase up to 3200A

MAIN DISTRIBUTION

PCC (Power Control Centre)

Main incoming plant switchboards with Air Circuit Breakers (ACBs), digital multifunction analyzers, and insulated heavy copper busbars.

• Capacity: Up to 6300A Main Busbars

• Structure: 2mm CRCA heavy-gauge modular

POWER FACTOR CORRECTION

APFC (Power Factor Panel)

Automatic reactive power compensation maintaining power factor above 0.98 with detuned harmonic filter reactors.

• Capacity: 25 kVAR to 1000 kVAR

• Harmonic Filtration: 7% / 14% detuned

VARIABLE SPEED DRIVES

VFD (Drive Panel)

Variable speed regulation with thermal cooling, line chokes, and EMC noise filters for pump, fan, and conveyor applications.

• Drive Ratings: 0.75 kW to 315 kW

• Bypass: Integrated DOL/Star-Delta changeover

OEM BESPOKE

Custom Industrial Control Panels

Tailor-made dimensions, stainless steel SS304/SS316 enclosures, and plug-and-play Harting connectors for machinery OEMs.

• Enclosures: IP65 / IP66 / NEMA 4X options

• Engineering: Built to client physical envelope
